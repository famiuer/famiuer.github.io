Surface Wave Shoaling

Linear wave theory over a sloping seabed. Offshore wave energy moves shoreward, wavelength shortens, and wave height grows as the depth decreases.

Linear Wave Model
\[ \omega^2 = gk\tanh(kh) \]
\[ H(x) \asymp H_0 \sqrt{\frac{c_{g,0}}{c_g(x)}} \]
Breaking option: \( H \le \gamma h \) with \( \gamma = 0.78 \).
Enable breaking limiter + foam

This first version keeps the physics intentionally simple: monochromatic linear waves, shoaling from group-velocity change, and an optional breaker cap near the coast.
