Reduced Wake Dynamics

This rebuild does not claim to solve the full Navier-Stokes equations in real time. Instead, it uses a classical reduced-order picture: the wake onset is controlled by a complex Stuart-Landau amplitude, while the top panel advects streamlines through a cylinder-flow field modulated by an alternating vortex street.

The single slider is the Reynolds number \(Re\). It changes the linear growth rate \(\mu\), so below onset the wake amplitude decays back to zero, while above onset the amplitude saturates to a stable periodic orbit.

$$\frac{dA}{dt} = (\mu + i\omega_0)A - |A|^2A, \qquad \mu = c\,(Re-Re_c)$$

In polar form \(A = r e^{i\theta}\), the amplitude obeys \(\dot r = \mu r - r^3\). So for \(\mu < 0\), the origin is stable; for \(\mu > 0\), the wake settles onto a limit cycle of radius \(r_\star = \sqrt{\mu}\). The flow panel is then driven by an alternating downstream vortex pattern whose strength is tied to \(|A|\).
